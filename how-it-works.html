HOW IT WORKS

A clear process from requirement to connection.

We focus on collecting the right information before connecting a parent/student with a teacher.

01. Submit Requirement

Parent/guardian shares class, subject, mode, location, timing and learning needs.

02. Review & Match

Teacher details and the student requirement are compared for suitability and availability.

03. Connect

Suitable options can be discussed through the consulting process and WhatsApp coordination.

04. Confirm Before Starting

Parent and teacher should confirm fee, timing, syllabus, mode, location, trial/demo arrangement and cancellation terms.

05. Begin Tuition

Only after both sides are comfortable and the final teaching arrangement is mutually agreed.
