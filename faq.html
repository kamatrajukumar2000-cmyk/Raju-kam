FAQ • SAFETY • CLARITY

Clear expectations prevent misunderstandings.

Parents, guardians and teachers should read these points before confirming a tuition arrangement.

Does teacher registration guarantee a student or job?

No. Registration only submits a teacher profile for consideration. A connection depends on suitable student requirements, availability and mutual agreement.

Does a parent request guarantee a particular teacher?

No. Teacher availability and suitability must be confirmed before any final arrangement.

Who confirms the tuition fee?

The parent/student and teacher should mutually confirm the fee and payment terms before classes begin.

What should be confirmed before starting?

Class, subject/syllabus, mode, location, timing, fee, trial/demo arrangement, cancellation/rescheduling terms and communication expectations.

Are minor students allowed to contact teachers independently?

Parent/guardian involvement is recommended and should be maintained for minor students, especially for home tuition arrangements.

Are teacher phone numbers shown publicly?

No. The platform is designed to avoid unnecessarily publishing personal contact details. Initial coordination can be handled through the consulting contact.

Is the final tuition arrangement made by the platform?

Sweksha Fondation Club provides teacher-student consulting and connection support. The final teaching arrangement, fee and schedule must be mutually agreed by the relevant parent/student and teacher.
