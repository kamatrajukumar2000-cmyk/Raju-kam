FAQ & SAFETY

Important questions, answered clearly.

Do you guarantee a teacher?

No. We help connect suitable requirements and teacher information. Final selection is between the parent/student and teacher.

What should parents confirm?

Class, subject, syllabus, teaching mode, location, timing, fee, trial class and cancellation terms.

What should teachers provide?

Accurate qualification, experience, subjects, classes, availability, location and contact information.

Is payment made through the website?

Not in this initial version. Payment terms should be mutually agreed between the parties.

How do I report a problem?

Contact Raju Kamat by WhatsApp or phone using the Contact page.
