HOW IT WORKS

Simple, transparent and clear.

We focus on collecting the right information before a parent and teacher connect.

01

Parent request

Parent/guardian tells us class, subject, location, mode, timing and requirement.

02

Teacher details

Teacher shares qualification, experience, classes, subjects and availability.

03

Review & suitability

Information is checked for suitability. A connection is not a guarantee of selection.

04

Confirm terms

Parent and teacher directly confirm fee, timing, trial class, location and other terms.
